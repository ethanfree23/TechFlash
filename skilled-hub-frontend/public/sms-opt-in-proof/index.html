PURPOSE OF THIS PAGE

This page documents the SMS opt-in process used in the TechFlash Facebook and Instagram Technician Questionnaire.

This page does not independently collect consent. It documents the opt-in flow used in the published TechFlash Meta Instant Form.

OPT-IN PROCESS

  1. A prospective technician sees a TechFlash advertisement on Facebook or Instagram.
  2. The user opens the TechFlash Technician Questionnaire.
  3. The user answers technician-qualification questions.
  4. The user enters or confirms their name, email address, and telephone number.
  5. Before submitting, the user is presented with separate SMS consent choices for marketing and non-marketing messages.
  6. Each SMS checkbox is optional.
  7. Neither SMS checkbox is selected by default.
  8. The user may submit the form without agreeing to either type of SMS.
  9. The user may select the marketing consent, informational consent, both, or neither.
  10. TechFlash sends SMS only in accordance with the consent selected and applicable law.
  11. The form provides access to the TechFlash Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
  12. Consent is recorded for TECHFLASH INC. d/b/a TechFlash and is not sold, rented, assigned, or transferred to another company for independent marketing.
