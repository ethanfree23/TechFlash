This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, retain, and protect personal information when you use the TechFlash website, mobile application, communications, forms, advertisements, technician network, business tools, and related services collectively referred to as the "Services."

By accessing or using the Services, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. Your use of the Services is also governed by the TechFlash Terms of Service at:

https://www.techflash.app/terms-of-service
